Table Timer

A game clock for the whole table.

Put one phone in the middle of the table. Everyone gets their own time, and a tap passes the turn to the next player.

Get it on the App Store Try it in your browser

2 to 8 players

Name everyone, give them a color, and drag them into seating order.

Tap to pass

The screen turns the current player's color and drains as their time runs down.

Who goes first?

Spin the list to pick a starting player without changing the seats.

Out of time?

The clocks stop. Remove the player, add time, or let them play on in overtime.

On your Lock Screen

See whose turn it is and their countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Nothing to sign up for

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your names and settings stay on your phone.
