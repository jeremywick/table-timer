Common questions

How do I pass the turn?

Tap anywhere on the big colored tile when your turn is done. The first tap starts the first player's clock.

Someone tapped by mistake.

Tap Undo. It goes back to the previous player, and the seconds since the tap move back onto the right clock.

How do I change the turn order?

On the setup screen, drag a player by the handle on the right. Who goes first? picks a random starting player and keeps everyone in their seats. Shuffle mixes everyone up.

What happens when someone runs out of time?

Every clock stops. You can remove that player, give them one or five more minutes, or let them keep playing in overtime, where their clock counts below zero.

There's no sound.

Check the speaker button next to Pause, and your volume. With sound on, Table Timer plays even when your iPhone is on silent.

How do I see the countdown on my Lock Screen?

Start a game, then lock your phone. If iOS asks, allow Live Activities for Table Timer. You can change this in Settings > Table Timer.

Does it work without an iPhone?

Yes. Play in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer.
